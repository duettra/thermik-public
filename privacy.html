Thermik privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Thermik shows your paragliding flights as a 3D replay over real terrain. It reads the flight files you give it and keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Your flights stay on your phone

  • Thermik reads IGC, GPX, KML, KMZ and ZIP files that you import, open or share to it. Files you pick or share are copied into the app's private storage; a folder you import is read where it is.
  • Your logbook (sites, glider names, corrections) is stored only on your phone.
  • Thermik never uploads your flight files or your logbook anywhere.
  • Thermik does not use your phone's location. It has no location permission.

What leaves your phone, and when

Only when you tap Download on a flight, Thermik fetches terrain, aerial imagery and place names for the area around that flight. These requests contain the map tiles or the rectangle covering the flight area, which reveals roughly where the flight took place. Like any web request, they also carry your IP address. They go, over encrypted HTTPS, to:

  • AWS Terrain Tiles (Amazon Web Services) — elevation
  • EOX IT Services — Sentinel-2 cloudless imagery
  • For flights in these countries: basemap.at (Austria), swisstopo (Switzerland), Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung (Bavaria), IGN (France) — aerial photos
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.private.coffee) — names of peaks, towns and passes

These providers answer the request; Thermik sends them nothing else. Their own privacy policies govern their server logs. Downloaded tiles are kept on your phone so the flight works offline afterwards.

Sharing

When you share a picture, video or file (GPX, KMZ, IGC), Thermik hands it to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you do this.

Deleting your data

Downloaded terrain and imagery can be cleared in Settings → Storage. Uninstalling Thermik, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes the logbook and everything else the app stored. Your original flight files in folders you imported are never changed or deleted.

Children

Thermik is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
