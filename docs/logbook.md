# Logbook & activities

## Tabs, search and sort

The logbook groups your tracks in tabs: **All**, **Flights**, **Hikes**, **Bike**, **Ski**
(tabs appear once the logbook holds more than flights). Search finds sites, gliders, pilots
and notes; sort by date, activity, pilot, site, distance or duration. With several pilots on one phone, the
pilot chip filters by pilot.

## Activities

Thermik guesses the activity of every track:

- **Paragliding** — any IGC from a vario, and GPS tracks that glide and circle.
- **Hike & Fly** — walked up at least 10 minutes and 100 m, then flew. The row shows both
  parts; the replay colours the walk and the flight separately.
- **Hike**, **Bike / MTB**, **Ski tour** — from GPX tracks: the file's own type when it has
  one, else the speed and the up-and-down profile.

A wrong guess is one tap to fix in **Edit flight → Activity**.

## Editing a flight

Tap the title on a flight to open **Edit flight**:

- title, site name (just this flight, or every flight launched there), glider, pilot, notes;
- **Launch and landing** — move the detected launch and landing, or **Auto-detect** again;
- **Hide from logbook** — hidden flights stay available under **Show skipped recordings**;
- **Delete flight** — removes it from the logbook. A flight imported from a folder stays in
  your folder, and Sync will not bring it back.

The original file is never changed.
