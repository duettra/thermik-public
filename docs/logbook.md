# Logbook & activities

## Search, sort and filter

Search finds sites, titles, gliders, pilots, notes and file names. The **sort** chip orders by
date, activity, pilot, site, distance or duration. The **filter** chip (funnel) shows one kind
(Flights, Hikes, Bike rides, Ski tours, Drives, Planes) or, with several pilots on one phone, one
pilot; ✕ clears it. The newest year shows your totals. In landscape it all fits on one line.

## Activities

Thermik guesses the kind of every track:

- **Flight**: any IGC from a vario, and GPS tracks that glide and circle.
- **Hike & Fly**: walked up at least 10 minutes and 100 m, then flew. The row shows both
  parts; the replay colours the walk and the flight separately.
- **Hike**, **Bike ride**, **Ski tour**, **Drive** (car, motorbike, bus): from GPX tracks, by the
  file's own type when it has one, else by speed and the up-and-down profile.
- **Plane**: a GPS track that climbs or sinks at 3 m/s or more while moving at 100 km/h or more,
  e.g. a Flightradar24 KML of an airline trip.

A wrong guess is one tap to fix in **Edit activity → Activity**.

## The row menu

Each row's **⋮** opens **Edit activity** and **Export**. Swipe a row left for **Hide** and **Delete**;
**Undo** brings a hidden activity straight back.

## Editing an activity

Tap the title of an open activity (✎), or **Edit activity** in the row menu:

- title, site name (just this activity, or every one launched there), glider, pilot, notes;
- **Start**: the date and time, if the device clock was wrong. The whole track moves, the
  exports too; the original file stays as it is. **Auto-detect** goes back to the logged time;
- **Launch and landing**: move the detected launch and landing, or **Auto-detect** again;
- **Hide from logbook**: hidden activities stay under **Settings → Appearance → Show hidden
  activities**;
- **Delete activity**: removes it from the logbook. One imported from a folder stays in your
  folder, and Sync will not bring it back.

The original file is never changed.
