# Getting started

## Install

Get Thermik from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thermik.paragliding)
(Android 8 or newer). It is free, with no account and no ads.

## Try the sample flight

On an empty logbook, tap **Try a sample flight** (Emberger Alm, Austria, 48 min) to see
everything before you import your own.

## Import your activities

Tap **+** in the logbook:

- **Import a folder**: pick the Skytraxx `FLIGHTS` folder (connect the vario over USB-C)
  or a copy of it. Subfolders are included; nothing in the folder is changed. Later, **Sync**
  (↻) picks up new flights from the same folder.
- **Import files**: single IGC, GPX, KML or KMZ files, or a ZIP of them.

You can also open or share a track file to Thermik from another app (Files, WhatsApp, e-mail).

Thermik recognises duplicates, detects launch and landing, guesses the kind of each activity
(flight, hike, bike ride, ski tour, drive, plane) and hides recordings too short to be anything
(**Settings → Appearance → Show hidden activities**). Nothing is deleted.

## Download the terrain

The first time you open an activity, Thermik offers **Download** for the terrain and aerial
photos around it (the size is shown). After that it works fully offline. In Austria,
Switzerland, Bavaria and France Thermik uses the national aerial photos; elsewhere Sentinel-2.
