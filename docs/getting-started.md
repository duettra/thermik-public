# Getting started

## Install

Thermik is in closed testing on Google Play (Android 8 or newer). Joining takes two steps:

1. Join the [Thermik tester group](https://groups.google.com/g/thermik-testers) with your Google account.
2. Open the [testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.thermik.paragliding), tap
   **Become a tester**, then install from the Play Store link on that page.

Use the same Google account as your Play Store. If Play says "item not found", step 2 is
missing or Google needs 15–30 minutes to switch it on.

## Try the sample flight

On an empty logbook, tap **Try a sample flight** (Emberger Alm, Austria, 48 min) to see
everything before you import your own.

## Import your flights

Open the **⋮** menu in the logbook:

- **Import flight folder** — pick the Skytraxx `FLIGHTS` folder (connect the vario over USB-C)
  or a copy of it. Subfolders are included; nothing in the folder is changed. Later, **Sync**
  (↻) picks up new flights from the same folder.
- **Import files or ZIP** — single IGC, GPX, KML or KMZ files, or a ZIP of them.

You can also open or share a flight file to Thermik from another app (Files, WhatsApp, e-mail).

Thermik recognises duplicates, detects launch and landing, and tucks away recordings too short to
be a flight (see **Show skipped recordings**). Nothing is deleted.

## Download the terrain

The first time you open a flight, Thermik offers **Download** for the terrain and aerial photos
around it (the size is shown). After that the flight works fully offline. In Austria,
Switzerland, Bavaria and France Thermik uses the national aerial photos; elsewhere Sentinel-2.
