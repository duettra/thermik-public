# Sharing & export

## Picture and video

In the activity's details, **Share → Picture** or **Video** opens the options:

- **Format**: 9:16 Story, 1:1 Square, 16:9 Wide.
- **Length** (video): 30, 45 or 60 s.
- **Camera** (video):
  - **Orbit**: one slow turn around the whole track while it draws itself.
  - **Chase**: rides along behind the glider (or the hiker's pin).
  - **Cinematic**: a wide orbit, a dive into the chase, then a pull-out at the end.
- **Stats**: up to four of duration, altitude, speed, climb, ascent, distance. Thermik remembers
  them per activity; hikes default to ascent, rides to speed.

Tap **Create**; the video renders on the phone (it takes a little while; Back cancels it), then
Android's share sheet opens. With **Settings → Export & sharing → Caption with shared pictures
and videos** on, a short text with site, date and the main numbers goes along.

## Export

In the details, or **Export** in the logbook row's ⋮ menu:

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **GPX** | The track with real altitude (Fused unless you choose another), for any map or sports app. |
| **KMZ** | For Google Earth, the track at its true height. |
| **IGC** | The original file, byte for byte. Its signature stays valid for XContest and DHV-XC. |

The ⓘ next to the buttons explains each format. GPX and KML imports have no original IGC, so IGC
is not offered for them.
