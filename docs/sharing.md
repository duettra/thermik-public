# Sharing & export

## Picture and video

In the flight's sheet, **Share → Picture** or **Video** opens the options:

- **Format** — 9:16 Story, 1:1 Square, 16:9 Wide.
- **Length** (video) — 10, 20, 30 or 60 s.
- **Camera** (video) — **Orbit**: one slow turn around the whole flight while the track draws
  itself. **Chase**: rides along behind the glider (or the hiker's pin).
- **Stats** — up to four of time, altitude, speed, climb, ascent, distance. Thermik remembers
  them per activity; hikes default to ascent, rides to speed.

Tap **Create**; the video renders on the phone (it takes a little while), then Android's share
sheet opens. With **Settings → Export & sharing → Caption with shared pictures and videos** on, a short text with site, date
and the main numbers goes along.

## Export

| Button | What you get |
|---|---|
| **GPX** | The track with real altitude (fused by default, set in Settings), for any map app. |
| **KMZ** | For Google Earth, the track at its true height. |
| **IGC** | The original file, byte for byte. Its signature stays valid for XContest and DHV. |

GPX and KML imports have no original IGC, so IGC is not offered for them.
