# The 3D view

## Moving around

- **Drag** to orbit, **pinch** to zoom, **two fingers** to pan.
- **Compass** (tap) turns the view to north. **Reset view** frames the whole flight again.
- The small **locator** map shows where in the world the flight is.

## Replay

The controls work like a tape deck:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| ▶ / ❚❚ | Play / pause. Play starts the chase view, the camera riding behind the glider. |
| ▶▶ / ◀◀ | Fast forward / rewind; each tap doubles the pace, up to 8×. Play returns to normal pace. |

Drag across the altitude profile in the sheet to jump anywhere. Touching the 3D view hands the
camera back to you. The replay pace is set per activity in **Settings → Map & 3D**
(flights 15×, hikes and ski tours 120×, bike rides 60× real time by default).

## Colours

**Colour by** in the sheet: **Climb rate** (blue sink → white → yellow → red climb, ±3 m/s),
**Altitude**, **Speed**, or **Steepness** (on the ground). The legend on screen shows the scale
and where the marker is on it. Hikes, rides and ski tours start with speed.

## Altitude

Your vario logs two altitudes. Pressure altitude is smooth but referenced to the standard
atmosphere, often 10–200 m off; GPS altitude has the right reference but is noisy. Thermik's
default, **Fused**, takes the shape from the barometer and the level from GPS. **GNSS** and
**Baro** show the raw values; Baro warns how far off it is on this flight.

## Hikes and GPX tracks

On foot a violet pin marks you instead of the glider. A GPX route without times is walked at
4 km/h; a track without heights follows the terrain.
