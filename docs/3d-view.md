# The 3D view

## Moving around

- **Drag** to orbit, **pinch** to zoom, **two fingers** to pan.
- **Compass** (tap) turns the view to north.
- The small **locator** map shows where in the world the activity is; tap it to zoom out to the
  whole activity.

## Replay

The controls work like a tape deck:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| ▶ / ❚❚ | Play / pause. Play starts the chase view: the camera follows the glider (or the hiker's pin). |
| ▶▶ / ◀◀ | Fast forward / rewind; each tap doubles the pace. Play returns to normal pace. |

While it plays, the camera stays locked on the glider:

- **drag** to look from the side or from above; the view turns with the glider;
- **pinch** to come closer or move away;
- **double-tap** to go back straight behind;
- **compass** to look north while it keeps following.

**Pause** frees the camera; a drag then orbits round the glider. Drag across the altitude profile
to jump anywhere. The replay pace is set per activity in **Settings → Map & 3D → Replay speed**
(flights 15×, hikes and ski tours 120×, bike rides 60×, drives and planes 120× real time by default).

## Colours

**Colour by** in the details: **Climb rate** (blue sink → white → yellow → red climb, ±3 m/s),
**Altitude**, **Speed**, **Steepness** (on the ground) or **Plain** (one green line). The legend
on screen shows the scale and where the marker is on it. Flights start with climb rate; hikes,
rides and drives with Plain. The ⓘ next to it explains every option.

## Altitude

Your vario logs two altitudes. Pressure altitude is smooth but referenced to the standard
atmosphere, often 10–200 m off; GPS altitude has the right reference but is noisy. Thermik's
default, **Fused**, takes the shape from the barometer and the level from GPS. **GNSS** and
**Barometric** are the alternatives. The choice sets the altitude in GPX and KMZ exports; the
numbers on screen always use Fused.

## Hikes, rides and drives

On foot a violet pin marks you instead of the glider. Hikes, bike rides, ski tours and drives are
drawn on the terrain, and their profile follows the ground; the numbers keep the logged altitude.
A GPX route without times is walked at 4 km/h. A plane trip stays in the air, with the pin
instead of the glider.

## Landscape

Turn the phone: the details move to a side panel next to a full-height 3D view. The **‹** tab
hides the panel.
