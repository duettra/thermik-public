# Changelog

## 0.8 — 13–14 October 2026

- While playing, the camera stays on the glider: drag to look from the side or from above, pinch
  to zoom, double-tap to go back behind; Pause frees it (0.8.4).
- Landscape: the activity screen gets a side panel and a full-height 3D view; the logbook fits
  on one line per activity (0.8.2, 0.8.3).
- The logbook and the activity screen show the same numbers; bigger text sizes fit (0.8.1).
- Videos render safely through a screen rotation, and Back cancels them.
- Confirmations before clearing tiles, replacing the logbook or discarding edits; Undo after
  Hide. Backup and restore are more robust.

## 0.7 — 11–12 October 2026

- **Cinematic** video camera: a wide orbit, a dive into the chase, a pull-out. Calmer chase
  videos; lengths 30, 45 or 60 s.
- Edit the start date and time of any activity; the whole track and its exports move with it.
- Every logbook row shows its kind's icon; durations with units ("9 min 29 s").
- Sort and one filter chip (activity and pilot) on one row.
- One set of words everywhere: activities, flights, logbook.

## 0.6 — 9–11 October 2026

- **Drive**: car, motorbike and bus rides get their own kind and replay speed.
- Hikes, rides and drives are drawn on the terrain, and so is their profile.
- ⓘ explainers for Colour by, Altitude source and the export formats.
- Logbook: **+** to import, a filter chip for the activity, hidden activities under
  Settings → Appearance.
- Thousands separators everywhere, German number style in the German app.
- A new instrument look (B612 numbers, muted gold), less battery use in the 3D view, safer
  handling of unusual files.

## 0.5 — 30 September – 2 October 2026

- New colours and logo; the empty logbook shows a start page with the sample flight.
- **Plain** colouring (one green line), the default for hikes, rides and drives.
- Tracks on foot are never drawn below the terrain.
- Logbook rows get a ⋮ menu (Edit, Export) and swipe left for Hide and Delete.
- GPX and KML files from more apps import.

## 0.4.0 — 29 September 2026

- Share options: format (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), video length, orbit or chase camera, and which stats
  to show, remembered per activity.
- Hikes, rides and ski tours: the walker's pin shows in videos and pictures, with ascent or
  speed instead of climb rate.
- Captions fit the activity (🥾 🚴 ⛷️), no glider on non-flights.
- Smoother marker in shared videos.

## 0.3.0 — 29 September 2026

- Hikes, bike rides, ski tours and hike & fly: recognised automatically (change it in Edit),
  logbook tabs per activity.
- More GPX files import: ApexGPS, planned routes without times, tracks without heights.
- Smoother replay; on foot a pin replaces the glider, and hikes play at their own speed.
- The bottom panel fits every phone, with bigger text too.

## 0.2.0 — 28 September 2026

- Colour legend on the 3D view and in shared media.
- Tape-deck replay controls (◀◀ ▶ ▶▶).
- Full flight editing: title, site, glider, pilot, notes, launch and landing, hide, delete.
- Logbook search and sort; several pilots on one phone.
- Backup & restore in one ZIP.
- Sample flight to try on an empty logbook.

## 0.1.0 — 25 September 2026

- First release: 3D replay over real terrain, offline; logbook with folder import from the
  Skytraxx; GPX, KMZ and original IGC export; share picture and video; national aerial photos
  in Austria, Switzerland, Bavaria and France; settings.
