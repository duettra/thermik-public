# Thermik user guide

Thermik replays your paragliding flights in 3D over the real terrain, offline, from the files
your vario writes. It also shows hikes, bike rides, ski tours and hike & fly.

- [**Getting started**](/docs/getting-started/) — install, import from your Skytraxx, first flight.
- [**The 3D view**](/docs/3d-view/) — gestures, replay controls, colours, altitude.
- [**Logbook & activities**](/docs/logbook/) — tabs, search, editing, hike & fly.
- [**Sharing & export**](/docs/sharing/) — pictures, videos, GPX, KMZ, the original IGC.
- [**Backup & restore**](/docs/backup/) — one ZIP for a new phone.
- [**FAQ**](/docs/faq/)
- [**Changelog**](/docs/changelog/)
